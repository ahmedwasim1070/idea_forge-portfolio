## 2. What the app is

YT Video is a Windows desktop client for YouTube. It puts YouTube, YouTube Music, and YouTube Kids in a dedicated application window with its own chrome and controls, and adds the things a browser tab cannot:

- Ad blocking
- A pop-out player
- Background play, with desktop-wide media keys
- Video downloading

The app is a viewer. It does not host, mirror, re-serve, or index video content. Content is delivered from YouTube's own services directly to your computer, in the same general way as when you use YouTube in a web browser.
